Generated by `python -m trustissues.nba.fairness` on the 2026-09-28 data. Protected attributes come from the CRM and are joined back **only** for this report (`sources.protected_attributes`, readable only by `nba/fairness.py`); they are not model features and no rule or decision uses them. Only aggregates are shown; groups under 100 people are left out. Vulnerability is not compared: it is a safeguard that deliberately changes treatment (POL-COLL-004 §4.5).

This is a screen for review, not a legal test. A flag means a difference large enough to look into: calibration more than 0.05 away from overall, AUC more than 0.05 below overall, a lowest / highest group ratio below 0.8 (the four-fifths rule of thumb), contact more than 25% away from what the group's risk predicts, or features that predict an attribute with AUC 0.6 or more. A model flag also needs the difference to be at least 1.96 standard errors, and an AUC flag 100 rows of each outcome, so small groups are shown but not flagged on noise. Groups differ in how often they break promises or fail to cure, so different selection rates are expected (each flag shows the groups' actual rates next to them); calibration within each group, and accuracy, are what say a score treats a group fairly.

## 4. People with no CRM record

Not a protected group, so left out of the screens above, but worth knowing: identity resolution found these people only in the card, loan or deposit systems (or could not tie the case to anyone), so there are no protected attributes to compare. They are rows of every table above, labelled *not in CRM*. Cases whose CRM ID does not exist (data quality check R1) are no longer here when identity tied them to a person.

- Promise break risk: 101 holdout rows; actual 57.4%; our score's gap +0.016 (overall +0.019), the bank's -0.014; 30.7% in our riskiest 20%.
- Case risk of not curing in 30 days: 195 holdout rows; actual 86.2%; our score's gap -0.029 (overall +0.005), the bank's -0.307; 25.6% in our riskiest 20%.
- Morning queue: 249 cases, 0.0% get any contact and 100.0% wait. With no CRM record there is no consent on any channel, so the rules allow nothing but waiting (default deny).
